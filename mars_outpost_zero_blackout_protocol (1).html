OUTPOST ZERO · COMMAND DESK-01

WE LOST CONTACT WITH EARTH

It is Mars Day 412. A huge dust storm has cut off contact with Earth and your team outside. You are in charge of Outpost Zero. Keep the base running for five days and send a rescue signal. Check the vent cameras for smoke. Use the dust monitor to find areas with unsafe air. The monitor uses battery while it is on, so switch it off when you are done.

CHOOSE GAME MODE

Standard mode: the original five-day mission.

A personal api.nasa.gov key provides higher request limits. The key is used in this browser session and is not saved by the game.

Live Mode checks NASA DONKI solar events and the latest accessible Curiosity REMS weather report. If a live feed is unavailable, that event type is not fabricated.

CHOOSE DIFFICULTY

Your name will appear in the game.

YOUR JOB
• Check the vent cameras. Look for smoke, then fix the broken vent.
• Check the dust monitor. Find dusty areas and seal them.
• Save battery. Fixing things and using the dust monitor take power.
• On Day 5, keep at least 12% battery and send your rescue signal. If the battery runs out, the base will shut down.
